1. Information used by the app

BluRaah does not require a hosted account, email address or phone-number registration. Each installation creates a cryptographic identity on your device. The app handles:

  • Your profile and contacts: your chosen display name, optional profile photo, public identity/contact card, saved contacts and approval or blocking preferences.
  • Your conversations: message text, drafts, photos and files you choose to send or receive.
  • Communication records: sender and destination identifiers, message identifiers, timestamps, delivery and unread states, transfer progress and routing information.
  • Relay data: encrypted message packets temporarily carried for other participating devices when relaying is enabled.

The app uses this information to identify peers, establish connections, route and deliver messages, show conversations, prevent duplicates and apply your preferences. SamiDev Studio does not operate a central BluRaah message-storage server or a cloud history-recovery service.

2. What other devices can receive

BluRaah communicates using nearby Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connections. Your display name and public contact card can be distributed through participating devices, including devices beyond the person you are messaging. Profile thumbnails are exchanged with approved, directly connected compatible peers.

Message bodies and attachments are encrypted for the intended destination. Other participating phones can store and forward the encrypted packets. Relay devices can still observe routing identifiers, packet sizes and timing; encryption does not make network activity anonymous.

Recipients can read, copy, save, photograph or forward content they receive. Deleting content on your phone does not remove copies held by recipients or other devices. SamiDev Studio does not sell your message content or use it for advertising targeting.

3. Local storage and security

Identity keys are protected using Android Keystore. Message bodies, drafts, attachment chunks, contact cards and profile images are encrypted in the app's local storage. Operational metadata is not fully encrypted. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup of BluRaah's private app data are disabled.

These measures have limits. A compromised or unlocked device, a recipient's actions or exported copies can expose information. The protocol does not provide forward secrecy: compromise of identity keys may affect previously captured encrypted messages. No storage or communication system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Retention and deletion

Local conversations, saved contacts, profile information and settings remain on your device until removed through available controls or by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Some records, such as markers used to prevent deleted messages from reappearing, can remain after a message or chat is deleted.

Relay packets have a maximum configured lifetime of 24 hours. Expired packets become ineligible for forwarding and are cleaned up during app operation; an inactive device may retain stored bytes until cleanup runs. You can also clear carried copies using the app's controls.

You can delete local messages or chats, remove your profile photo, block a contact and pause Nearby. Blocking applies to an installation identity; it cannot identify the same person after they create a new identity. Removing a photo does not recall copies others have already saved.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes access to that installation's identity and history. There is no BluRaah cloud account to delete or server backup from which SamiDev Studio can restore your messages. Files you saved elsewhere and copies on other devices must be managed separately.

5. Permissions, photos and saved files

  • Nearby devices, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi: used to discover and communicate with participating phones. Disabling required connectivity pauses communication.
  • Location on older Android versions: Android requires location access and/or enabled location services for nearby discovery on certain versions. BluRaah does not build a history of GPS coordinates.
  • Notifications and foreground operation: used for unread-message alerts and visible ongoing nearby communication. You can manage permissions in Android settings and pause Nearby in the app.
  • Photos, files and camera: BluRaah uses system selection and save flows. Taking a photo opens a camera app with temporary access to a capture location. BluRaah does not request access to your phone's address book, SMS inbox or all files on the device.

Photo mode processes images and removes source image metadata during conversion. File mode preserves original file bytes, which may contain location or other metadata. Choose what you share carefully.

Copying a message places it on the system clipboard. Saving or exporting content creates copies outside BluRaah's encrypted private storage. Your chosen document, gallery, camera or cloud-storage provider may process or sync that content according to its own settings and policy.

6. Google Play services and diagnostics

BluRaah uses Google Play services Nearby Connections. Google documents collection of connection-performance information, including discovery latency, reliability and throughput, together with device model, country, build version and app package name. Google describes a control at Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics; its location can vary by device.

See Google's Nearby data-collection disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Offline message transport does not mean that Google Play services or your operating system never processes or sends diagnostics when connectivity is available.

The current BluRaah app does not include advertising, Firebase analytics, a SamiDev Studio analytics backend or automatic crash-report uploads to SamiDev Studio. This statement does not cover independent processing by Google, your device manufacturer, your operating system or providers you choose to use.

7. When you contact us or visit this page

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use it to respond, investigate the reported issue and handle privacy or safety requests. Our email provider processes that correspondence. Please do not send passwords, identity keys or unrelated private conversations.

Support correspondence is retained as necessary to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us; legal or security obligations may require some records to be retained. We do not receive your local chat history automatically and cannot remotely erase copies from other phones.

Visiting this webpage requires an Internet connection. Its hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details and access times, to deliver and protect the page. SamiDev Studio has not added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or submission forms to this page. Email and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own.

8. Children

BluRaah is a general communication tool and is not designed specifically for children. We do not request dates of birth or operate a central profile-registration database. If you believe a child has provided personal information to SamiDev Studio through support correspondence, contact us so we can review the concern and address it as required.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose what to put in your profile, whom to connect with, what to send, whether to help relay messages, and when to pause nearby communication. You can revoke Android permissions, use local deletion controls and manage Google's diagnostics setting.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of information held by SamiDev Studio, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Send requests to developer.samikhan@gmail.com. We may request reasonable information to verify and respond to your request. These requests do not give us access to messages stored only on your phone or another user's device.
